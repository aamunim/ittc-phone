ITTC Phone is the phone app for the admission team of ITTC. It works with ITTC Admission Tracker, the institute's lead and admission app.

📞 A complete phone app
• Calls, recent calls, contacts, favourites, dial pad with name search
• Dual SIM, speed dial, blocked numbers, call screen with hold, merge and audio controls
• Fast: recent calls open at once

🎓 Built for admission calls (sign in with your ITTC account, or with ITTC Admission Tracker on the same phone)
• See the lead's name, course and stage on the call screen and in recent calls, even when the number is not saved in your contacts
• Leads tab: your open leads — today's calls, overdue, never called, hot — and the pipeline stages; super admins can see everyone's
• After a lead call: choose what happened, write a note, change the pipeline stage and set the next call — saved at once
• Find a lead from the dial pad by typing their name
• One row per number in recent calls, and a call history page for each number
• Lead page: notes, stage, next call, WhatsApp / SMS templates; add a new lead after a call
• Follow-up reminders, today's calls against your target, and "Call all" to work through a list

🔐 Private by design
• Your calls and contacts stay on your phone, with no ads or trackers
• Only calls with your institute's leads (and the results you save) go to your institute — personal calls never do
• Without signing in, ITTC Phone sends nothing over the internet

ITTC Phone is free software under the GNU GPL v3, based on Fossify Phone. Source code: https://github.com/aamunim/ittc-phone
