ITTC Phone is the phone app for the admission team of ITTC. It is a complete dialer that also knows your institute's leads, and works with ITTC Admission Tracker or on its own with your ITTC account.

📞 A complete phone app
• Dial pad on the main screen, with name search as you type
• Recent calls (one row per number, with a call history page), contacts, favourites, groups
• Contact page: every number with call, SMS and WhatsApp, emails, addresses, notes and history
• Dual SIM, speed dial, blocked numbers; call screen with hold, merge, speaker and Bluetooth
• Fast, even on 2 GB phones; text size and dark mode for the whole app

🎓 Built for admission calls
• See the lead's name, course and stage on the call screen and in recent calls, even when the number is not saved
• Leads tab: today's calls, overdue, never called, hot; My list / All list; a pipeline board
• After a lead call: what happened, a note, the stage and the next call date, saved at once
• Lead page: notes, stage, next call, fee, payments, papers and admission; hand a lead over to a colleague
• WhatsApp and SMS templates, with a choice of WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or a clone / dual app
• Copy any number in one tap
• Follow-up reminders, today's calls against your target, and "Call all" to work through a list
• Personal / ITTC mode: office hours and the office SIM keep work and personal calls apart

🔐 Private by design
• Your calls and contacts stay on your phone, with no ads or trackers
• Only calls with your institute's leads, and the work you save, go to your institute; personal calls never do
• Without signing in, ITTC Phone sends nothing over the internet

For ITTC staff: sign in with the account your institute gave you (menu → ITTC account).
